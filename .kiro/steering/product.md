# Product: Ghost Quoter 幽灵报价员
- Users: export sales staff at a Guangzhou furniture-hardware exporter (hinges, drawer slides, handles, knobs, struts, legs).
- Problem: overseas buyers still send RFQs by fax, plain-text email, or EDI X12 840. Re-keying costs hours and causes errors.
- Goal: RFQ in any legacy format → matched catalog lines → branded English quotation PDF → reply in the SAME format (fax PDF, text email, EDI 843).
- Category: Kiroween "Resurrection" — revive fax/EDI rather than force buyers onto a portal.
- Never invent prices: unmatched lines are flagged "NO MATCH - manual review". Catalog prices are EXAMPLE data.
- Tone: spooky Halloween UI, but the documents sent to buyers are strictly professional.
